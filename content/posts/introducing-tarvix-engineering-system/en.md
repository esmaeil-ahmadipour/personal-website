## What is the Tarvix Engineering System?

The Tarvix Engineering System is a codified set of architectural decisions, quality standards, and automation patterns. It represents what we learned solving complex problems across fintech, Web3, logistics, and ed-tech.

### Key Principles

- **Modular Domain Boundaries** — Feature-first organization with clean separation between domain logic, data access, and presentation.
- **Living Documentation** — Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) as first-class citizens.
- **Automated Quality Gates** — CI that enforces architecture rules before merge.
- **Reference Implementations** — Applied examples that clarify architectural decisions.

### Why This Matters

When architecture decisions are documented and enforced, teams stop debating the same questions. When quality is automated, human energy goes to product value, not firefighting.

The platform gives you visibility into how we build. The system provides patterns you can adapt. A shared foundation grounded in engineering reality.
